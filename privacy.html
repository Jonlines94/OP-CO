Privacy and data protection

How OP-CO Marine Ltd handles personal information.

Purpose

OP-CO is committed to handling information about employees, customers, stakeholders and other interested parties with care and confidentiality, and to treating individual rights with respect.

Scope

This policy applies to employees (including subsidiaries), contractors, consultants, partners and anyone acting on the company’s behalf who may access data. It also covers anyone who provides information to the company, such as job candidates, customers and suppliers.

Data covered

Any offline or online information that can identify a person, such as names, addresses, login details, photographs and financial data.

Our principles

  • Data should be accurate and up to date.
  • Data is collected fairly and lawfully, and processed within legal and ethical limits.
  • Data is protected from unauthorised access.
  • Data is not shared informally, kept longer than specified, transferred to places without adequate protections, or distributed beyond what the data owner agreed to — except for legitimate law-enforcement requests.

Our obligations to individuals

We tell people what data we collect, how it will be processed and who can access it. We have plans for lost, corrupted or compromised data, and we let people request changes, erasure, restriction or correction of their data.

Our actions

  • Restricting and monitoring access to sensitive data
  • Training staff
  • Securing networks and setting up breach-reporting procedures
  • Using contract clauses
  • Applying encryption, secure storage, shredding and backups

Enforcement

Breaches may lead to disciplinary action and possibly legal action.
