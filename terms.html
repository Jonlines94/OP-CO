OP-CO Marine Ltd — terms of business.

1. Liability

OP-CO accepts no responsibility for loss, damage or delay unless caused by its negligence or deliberate act. Customers must hold at least £2,000,000 third-party liability insurance and provide proof within 7 days of request.

2. Prices and Estimates

Prices are based on labour, materials and services, and are exclusive of VAT. Estimates are based on a superficial examination and exclude emergent work and extensions.

3. Delays

Completion times are estimates only and are not guaranteed.

4. Vessel Movements

OP-CO may move vessels and property for safety, security or business management.

5. Payment & Retention of Title

Invoices are due on the invoice date. Interest of 2% per month applies to sums unpaid 30 days after the due date. OP-CO may stop work on overdue accounts and holds a general lien over customer property. Title to goods stays with OP-CO until full payment.

6. Guarantee

Work is guaranteed for 6 months against defects due solely to poor workmanship, provided they are notified in writing within 21 days of discovery. Business customers’ recovery is generally limited to rectifying workmanship or replacing faulty articles. Statutory rights for consumers are unaffected.

7. Quality Standards

Work will be completed in reasonable accordance with the agreed specification and to a satisfactory quality.

8. Access to Premises / Work on the Vessel

Work on premises needs prior written consent, except minor routine running repairs. Work must comply with OP-CO’s health, safety, environmental and access policies.

9. Subcontracting

OP-CO may subcontract work and remains responsible for the subcontractor’s performance.

10. Authorisation

Instructions from an appointed skipper, engineer, manager, surveyor or beneficial owner are binding unless OP-CO has prior written notice otherwise.

11. Right of Sale

Under the Torts (Interference with Goods) Act 1977, OP-CO may sell goods a customer fails to collect after notice is given.

12. Notices

Notices are served by hand or first-class post.

13. Delivery

Delivery methods are chosen by OP-CO unless specified at order. Dispatch and delivery dates are estimates only.

14. Miscellaneous

English law applies, and disputes fall under the jurisdiction of the English Courts.
