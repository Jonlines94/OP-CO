Global refit and repair solutions

At OP-CO Marine, we operate as a mobile shipyard—an agile, highly skilled workforce capable of delivering refit, repair, and maintenance projects anywhere in the world.

Our international teams of experienced engineers, technicians, and tradespeople provide shipyard-quality services directly to vessels—whether at sea, in port, or during yard periods. We offer end-to-end solutions across engineering, interiors and deck disciplines alongside project management and consultancy services.

Our approach goes beyond short-term fixes. We support long-term strategic planning, enabling crew to begin their maintenance and repair programmes well in advance—even before vessel schedules and locations are confirmed. You are not limited to shipyard contractors.

About OP-CO

Built by seafarers.

OP-CO is a Southampton-based marine services company, founded in 2022 by Alex O’Connell and Tom Puddy. We provide refit and maintenance personnel who can be deployed anywhere in the world, as an all-in-one marine service provider.

Our management team has real seafaring experience. Alex joined the Merchant Navy at 16 and rose to Chief Engineer. Tom began in the luxury cruise sector, moved into private yachts, and became Chief Electrician.

We champion sustainable practices and environmental responsibility in everything we do.
